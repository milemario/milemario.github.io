How your final mark is calculated

50%

Class questionnaires

Short questionnaires are held at every class. Your points are added together across the semester and converted to a percentage of the available questionnaire points.

50%

Semester-closing test / exam

An ABCD multiple-choice test in the university’s Moodle system, using questions from the Cyber Anki question bank.

Final score = 0.5 × questionnaire percentage + 0.5 × test percentage.
Example: 80% in class questionnaires and 70% in the test gives 75% overall.

Conditions for completing the course

  • Attend classes and complete absence make-up work. The syllabus permits up to 25% absence for the full-time course; absences beyond that limit require the task set by the lecturer. Follow the make-up procedure below for every missed class.
  • Earn at least 50% of the available class questionnaire points. This is the syllabus minimum for obtaining the course signature.
  • Pass the semester-closing test. A passed closing test is also required for the course signature.
  • Achieve a passing final mark on the five-grade scale below. Credits require the course signature and a passed assessment.

The 50% questionnaire minimum is a completion condition. It is separate from the questionnaires’ 50% weight in your final mark.

Part-time attendance rule, where applicable

The syllabus allows one day of absence for the part-time course. Absences beyond that limit require make-up work assigned by the lecturer.

If you miss a class

For each missed class, complete the following make-up task:

  1. Watch the linked videos on Moodle.

    Use the videos linked for the topic of the class you missed.

  2. Prepare a brief summary.

    The syllabus requires a short summary of the material you watched, this doesn't have to be written.

  3. Present one video in 2–3 minutes.

    Give a short summary of one of the videos you watched, in person or through Teams or Zoom. Arrange the presentation with the lecturer.

Absence make-up fulfils the missed-class task. The syllabus’s minimum of 50% of available questionnaire points still applies.

What to prepare for the exam

The exam consists of questions from the Cyber Anki question bank, presented in ABCD multiple-choice format. Use Cyber Anki to practise the questions and understand the answers.

The assessed result is your Moodle closing-test result. Cyber Anki practice results do not add a separate component to your final mark.

Timing and correction: the syllabus places the full-time closing test in week 14. A failed closing test may be corrected once in the final teaching week. For the part-time course, the test is held in the last class and a correction is arranged with the lecturer.
