Prepare your senior briefing

Work as a team: use the source list to begin, then find at least one additional reliable source. Prepare a 2-minute briefing that states the issue, evidence, relevant rules and actors, your assessment, and next steps for the client meeting.

Label facts, interpretation, and open questions separately. If a point cannot be verified publicly, say what information you would request from the client. The printable handout includes the full research questions, note-taking worksheet, and review rubric.
