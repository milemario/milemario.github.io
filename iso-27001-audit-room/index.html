The Audit Room:
Can You Trust the Evidence?

You are junior auditors at Pannon Office Services Ltd. Review the three short policies, plan design and operating-effectiveness tests, and record evidence-based results. The Excel evidence workbook is distributed separately by your instructor.

Keep conclusions within the sample. This is a limited classroom audit of selected HR, access-management and change-management processes—not a certification decision. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (including Amendment 1:2024) is the framework. Use the three supplied Pannon policies as the criteria for this exercise. Their deadlines are company requirements, not deadlines prescribed by ISO.
