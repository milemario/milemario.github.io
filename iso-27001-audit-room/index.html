The Audit Room:
Can You Trust the Evidence?

Turn your team’s paper workpapers into a concise, evidence-based closing report. Enter what your team actually tested and observed. This page does not contain the answer key.

Keep the conclusion bounded. This exercise samples selected records for Pannon Office Services Ltd.; it is not a certification audit. ISO/IEC 27001 is the management-system framework. Specific control selection follows the organization’s risks, obligations, and Statement of Applicability. The supplied company policies establish the deadlines and technical expectations used in this exercise; those settings are not universal ISO deadlines.
Entries stay in this browser; they are not uploaded.

1. Audit details

Record the scope, criteria, and sample boundaries before summarizing results.

2. Audit workpapers

For each question, record the requirement, records selected, test, references, and reasoning. A policy states an expectation; operational evidence helps show implementation.

3. Three formal findings

Select your three strongest findings. Structure each as requirement → objective evidence → demonstrated gap. Describe a needed outcome if you suggest action; do not guess at root cause.

4. Focused evidence requests

Use up to two precise requests to resolve genuine uncertainty. State which record or explanation you need and why it matters.

5. Closing briefing

Prepare a two-minute summary: what you tested, key findings, a control that worked, uncertainties, and a limited conclusion.

Check that every factual statement in your report is traceable to a cited record. “More evidence needed” is valid when the package supports neither conformity nor a requirement breach.
